Travel Tips from the Taros

A few things we wish someone told us before travelling:

📱 Hold off buying a SIM card at the airport
Prices are usually much higher than elsewhere in the city.

💵 Bring some cash and exchange locally
You’ll often find better exchange rates outside the airport.

🔌 Pack a Type C (2-pin) plug adapter
A small item that’s easy to forget but very useful once you arrive.

🧥 Pack for warm days and cooler evenings
Marrakech in December can surprise you — sunshine during the day and a chill in the evening.

Most importantly: come ready to celebrate, relax, and enjoy everything Marrakech has to offer.

What should I pack?
Marrakech is generally warm and sunny, but temperatures can vary between day and evening, so we recommend packing accordingly.

Essentials include:

* Comfortable shoes for walking on uneven streets, cobblestones, and around the venue
* Sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat for daytime activities
* A light layer, shawl, or jacket for cooler evenings
* Breathable fabrics and lightweight clothing for daytime wear
* Formal attire for wedding events (please refer to the Dress Code section for inspiration)
* Any accessories or footwear needed for multiple event looks
* Swimwear if your accommodation has a pool or spa
* A small bag for excursions and day trips
* Personal medications and travel essentials
* A portable charger and travel adaptor if required

We recommend checking the forecast closer to your travel date and leaving a little extra luggage space for shopping in Marrakech.

What language do they speak in Morocco? 
Morocco’s official languages is Arabic however, in Marrakech, French is also widely spoken and commonly used in hotels, restaurants, and shops. Many people working in tourism also speak English, so guests should have no trouble getting around.
